Now that your Accounting Hub, General Ledger, Vendor Invoicing/AP, Courier Settlements, and Manual Phone Orders are all fully automated and operational, here are the most logical next steps to complete your enterprise system.
1. Vendor Stock Intake & Inventory Auto-Sync

Currently, when you record a Vendor Bill in accounting.php, it creates the financial liability (Accounts Payable) and inventory asset value.

    The Upgrade: Allow the accountant or warehouse manager to select which specific products/variants were received in that bill, and automatically increment the physical stock count in the products and product_variants tables.

2. Printable / PDF Vendor Statement of Account

Suppliers frequently ask for a running ledger statement to settle monthly bills.

    The Upgrade: Add a "Download Vendor Statement" (PDF/CSV) button inside the Vendors tab that generates a formal statement showing:

        Opening balance

        List of all invoices billed during a date range

        All payments disbursed (with cheque/bank reference numbers)

        Net outstanding closing balance

3. Stakeholder Dividend / Profit Distribution Vouchers

Now that your P&L calculates the Net Operating Profit and tracks stakeholder ownership percentages:

    The Upgrade: A module in the Capital tab to declare and disburse partner dividends or owner withdrawals. This automatically debits Retained Earnings (3030) and credits Bank (1010) without distorting operating expenses.

4. Admin Role & Permission Hardening for the Finance Team

When you onboard an external accountant or finance assistant:

    The Upgrade: Ensure they only have access to accounting.php and reports.php, while restricting them from modifying products, store settings, or customer passwords.
